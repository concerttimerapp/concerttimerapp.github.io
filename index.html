Concert Timer

For orchestra librarians

Concert Timer is a timing tool for orchestra librarians and personnel managers. Build the program, time each piece live, run a countdown intermission with a tuning note at the end, record encores, and share a PDF of the actual timings — scheduled start and end time included — moments after the final bow.
