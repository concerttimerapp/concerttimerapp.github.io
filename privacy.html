Privacy Policy

Concert Timer · Last updated June 2026

Concert Timer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

Your data stays on your device

Every concert, program, timing, and setting you create lives only on your iPhone. None of it is sent to me, to Apple, or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking.

No analytics, no third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics frameworks, and no third-party services that could gather information about you.

Sharing a PDF

When you choose to export a concert as a PDF and share it, that file goes only where you send it, using your device's own share sheet. Concert Timer plays no part beyond creating the file.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, write to michelleonard@mac.com.
